## Project Overview:
As an Associate Consultant, I have been assigned to a semester-long client 
engagement with Riverside Community Health Network (RCHN), a nonprofit 
health network operating six community clinics across the Chicago 
metropolitan area. RCHN is seeking to replace its aging patient intake and 
scheduling system, and this portfolio documents the project management 
deliverables I develop throughout the engagement — from project charter 
through planning, execution, and closure. I will cover project management artifacts consisted of initiation, planning, execution, monitoring and controlling, and project closure.
