## 1. Product Manager Role Analysis
The Product Manager and Project Manager roles are related but focused on different questions. 
The Product Manager's main focus is what the team essentially builds and develops and why it matters. This means researching users, defining a clear product vision, and prioritizing features being solely reliant on user need and business value. The Product Manager's team is anchored to the question: does the decision satisfy the user's needs and the goals of RCHN?
