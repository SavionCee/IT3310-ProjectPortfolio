**Consultant:** Savion Crockett
**Role:** Associate Consultant, Project Manager, NovaTech Consulting
**Engagement:** Riverside Community Health Network (RCHN)
**Date:** October 6, 2026
---
## Scope
### In Scope, Phase One
Patient intake workflow standardization across all six clinics
Appointment scheduling system replacement
Staff training for the new system at all six locations
Initial rollout support for 90 days post-launch

### Out of Scope, Phase One
Patient-facing online scheduling portal
Billing system integration with the new scheduling data
Executive reporting dashboard for clinic leadership
Mobile application for clinic staff

### Scope Justification
I chose billing system integration with the new scheduling data for excluding from phase One. Although it could add value, it is clear that it falls outside of the core problem in the project charter: inconsistent intake and scheduling workflows across RCHN's six clinics. Including it would expand project complexity as billing systems involve a different platform and compliance requirements. Additionally, this would prolong scheduling time since the team would need to do additional analyzation. It also would've increased cost and resource demands that require expertise, which may not be able to receive support in the engagement. Billing integration can be treated as a future phase for the project's change process. This in turn would allow NovaTech to focus on an achievable solution to RCHN's problem.

## SMART Objectives
### Objective 1
By the end of the 90-day initial rollout support period, IT support staff across all six RCHN clinics will see a documented decrease in unnecessary help-desk tickets related to unconfirmed appoint saves, as needed and verified by help-desk ticket tracking, confirmed with RCHN IT leadership.
**Connection to Product Vision:** This objective is informed by the product vision because the vision promised that the new system will reduce unnecessary support tickets and give IT staff confidence that patient data is accurately and consistently saved. The initial rollout support is confirmed in the scope, which is developed for 90 days post-launch.

### Objective 2
By completion of staff training at all six RCHN clinics, intake coordinators will be able to complete standardized intake and scheduling workflows utilizing the new system, as verified by sponsor-approved training completion records and workflow acceptance criteria.
**Connection to Product Vision:** This objective is informed by the product vision because research findings and customer needs identification discovered that intake coordinators need a more consistent, reliable workflow at all six clinics. Staff training for use of the new system is a confirmed in-scope item from the charter. 

---
## Planning Constraints
### Constraint 1
The solution in the project must be delivered across all six RCHN clinics, as mentioned in the project charter scope.
**Effect on Planning:** This constraints affects schedule and resource since training, rollout support, and implementation across all clinics produces scheduling difficulty and requires more staff support time compared to operating in a single location.

### Constraint 2
Major clinical workflow changes require approval from the RCHN Chief Medical Officer before moving forward.
**Effect on Planning:** This constraint affects schedule and risk. This is because a workflow-impacting decision builds over time for CMO review and approval before implementation. If this approval step is not discovered early in planning, unexpected delays can be expected later in the project and changes could become costly to make. 

---
## Assumptions
Assumption 1: We are assuming that clinic staff at all of RCHN's six clinics will have scheduled availability to attend staff training for the new intake and scheduling system without significantly reducing patient appointment capacity. If this is wrong, the training schedule will need to be extended, which would receive push back for the go-live timeline and could potentially increase the required number of training sessions, with both schedule and resources being affected.

Assumption 2: We are assuming that a pre-implementation baseline for help-desk tickets exists and can be established with RCHN IT leadership. If this is wrong, there would be inexistent confirmation that the new system reduced support tickets saved, and the objective's acceptance criteria would need to be coordinated again with the sponsor.

---
## Planning Foundation Synthesis
A planning decision I made this week was tying the SMART objective to reducing help-desk tickets related to unconfirmed appointment saves. This was informed by the Week 3 product vision statement and it stated that the new system would reduce unnecessary support tickets and essentially give staff confidence that patient data can be accurately and consistently saved. The additional judgment I had to make was avoiding a "make-up" date for the objective's timeframe and measurement criteria. The 90-day rollout support became a more simple and effective way to adhere to the objective. What still needs to be confirmed before the decision can be treated as final is figuring out if the pre-implementation ticket baseline actually exists, and confirming whether or not RCHN IT leadership agrees for it to measure the objective's success.
