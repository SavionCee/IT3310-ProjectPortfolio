# Project Charter — RCHN Intake & Scheduling System Replacement

**Prepared by:** Savion Crockett, Associate Consultant, NovaTech Consulting**
**Date:** September 13, 2026

## Business Need
RCHN's current patient intake and scheduling system is aging and outdated. The system's process is causing problems for staff and patients. There is also an issue with the disorganization of the system that the surrounding parties are not fond of, which results in the ineffectiveness of the system across its six clinics.
## Project Purpose
Replace RCHN's outdated patient intake and scheduling system with a unified platform across all six clinics.
## High-Level Scope
This project includes the following high-level areas of work:
1. Patient intake – replacing the current intake process with the new system
2. Appointment scheduling – implementing standardized scheduling across all clinics
3. Staff training – preparing clinic staff to use the new system
4. Initial rollout support – supporting the go-live transition at each of the six locationsb
## Success Criteria
Clinic staff can regularly schedule patients accurately and consistently across all six clinic locations, and leadership can generate reliable reporting from the new and improved system.
