## Agile Delivery Fit
The part of the RCHN intake and scheduling replacement that could benefit from iterative delivery is the feature and design of the system across RCHN's six clinics. This is included because as workflows have been inconsistent in various clinical locations, there is no guarantee the overall design will be as effective for the first time everywhere. Gathering feedback from intake coordinators and medical assistants would further improve the Sprint's goals by the design being delivered consecutively. One area that may still require stronger advance planning or control is the approval of the Chief Medical Officer when it comes to any major clinical workflow change. The CMO ultimately holds approval authority for operations and patient care, which means decisions must be evaluated before implementation.

## Scrum Team
**Product Owner:** NovaTech Product Manager
**Scrum Master:** NovaTech Consultant
**Developers:** NovaTech Implementation Team

### Rationale
Product Owner: NovaTech Product Manager. This Assignment makes sense because this role exemplifies the value of the product being delivered, as pertaining to the Scrum Guide. It aligns with the responsibilities of the Product Manager, which upholds product vision and consistent collaboration.

Scrum Master: NovaTech Consultant. This role is responsible for developing Scrum, coaching the team's self-management, ridding of any impediments, and pushing for more improvement.

Developers: NovaTech Implementation Team. Developers build increments for each Sprint Plan. NovaTech develops the replacement system, therefore the implementation staff conduct hands-on work. This makes sense as IT support staff at RCHN are the stakeholders and aren't part of the Scrum Team.

## Sprint Planning Reflection
These selected items form a coherent plan because Scheduling Workflow Prototype and Intake Form Standardization maintain the highest priority and tie to the Sprint goal of establishing a foundational workflow. The Sprint Communication update was added with an additional 2 points combining with 21 points. This ended up with 23 points because I included the points from the Scheduling Workflow Prototype, Intake Form Standardization, and Sprint Communication Update. The team should learn the Intake and Scheduling system design, as it is the main objective of the scope. One item I intentionally deferred was the Legacy Data Cleanup Review because it is considered low priority, something that is discovered later in the project, and as the replacement system is utilized. In doing so, it provides the team updates on the newly improved system and the Scrum Team would be able to document it.

## Scrum Master Reflection
### Obstacle and Response
One impediment that could affect progress toward the Sprint Goal would be not having much availability for feedback or review from clinic staff, due to intake coordinators patient volume being managed at RCHN's six clinics. The Scrum Master could help the Scrum Team by coordinating with clinic leadership and finding time to provide staff feedback without deciding how workflow should look.

### Preparing for Week 7
The Sprint Planning will help mem build and prioritize the product backlog by focusing first on the highly prioritized areas of work. Scheduling Workflow Prototype and Intake Form Standardization are the foundation to ensuring that other areas like the Clinic Staff Training Outline and Legacy Data Cleanup Review will be structured more professionally.
